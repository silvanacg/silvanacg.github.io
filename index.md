<p class="statement">
  I work at the intersection of atmospheric science, wildfire, and geomorphic hazards.
</p>

<p>
  I’m a Ph.D. Candidate in Geography focused on post-fire debris flows and hydroclimate extremes.
  I combine radar reflectivity, rain gauges, reanalysis, and climate projections to build statistical
  and machine-learning models for hazard assessment.
</p>

<p>
  I enjoy translating complex climate signals into spatially explicit insights that support resilience
  planning and evidence-based decision-making.
</p>
